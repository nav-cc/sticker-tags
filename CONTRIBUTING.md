# Contributing to Sticker Tags

English | [简体中文](#简体中文)

Thank you for helping. Sticker Tags is edited by Nav.cc. Anyone may propose; Nav.cc decides what is adopted and has the final say.

## How to propose

- **A new tag:** open an issue with the "New tag" form. Say in one or two sentences what the tag means about the object itself, and why it is needed. A glyph idea is welcome but not required.
- **A change to a tag:** open an issue with the "Change a tag" form: a clearer meaning, a better glyph, or a name in another language.
- **The wording of the standard:** open a pull request against `STANDARD.md` (English, canonical) or `STANDARD.zh-Hans.md`.

## What a tag must be

These come from the standard itself:

1. It states a fact about an object, never a judgment such as "good" or "recommended".
2. There are no negative tags: no "not free", no "no iOS app".
3. Its meaning explains the tag and nothing else. How a user decides which object gets the tag, or how it is shown, is left to the user.
4. Its glyph carries the meaning and can be recognized at a glance, down to 14 pixels: a square with rounded corners, vector graphics, no text characters, no company's logo.

## How decisions are made

- Nav.cc reviews every proposal and may adopt it, adapt it, postpone it or decline it. Nav.cc has the final say.
- An adopted tag gets the next free TID. Identifiers and TIDs are never reused.
- The files here are generated from Nav.cc's own copy of the standard. When a pull request is adopted, Nav.cc makes the change there and writes this repository out again, so the pull request may be closed rather than merged. The commit that carries the change credits its author.

## License of contributions

By opening an issue or a pull request, you agree that what you contribute is licensed under CC BY 4.0, the license of this repository, and that Nav.cc may publish it as part of the standard, which is credited to Nav.cc.

## 简体中文

感谢参与。Sticker Tags 由 Nav.cc 编辑。任何人都可以提议，是否采纳由 Nav.cc 决定，Nav.cc 有最终决定权。

### 怎么提议

- **新标签：** 用「New tag」表单开一个 Issue。用一两句话说明这个标签对对象本身意味着什么，以及为什么需要它。有图形设想更好，没有也可以。
- **修改已有标签：** 用「Change a tag」表单开一个 Issue：更清楚的含义、更好的图形，或者某种语言的名称。
- **标准的文字：** 对 `STANDARD.md`（英文，为准）或 `STANDARD.zh-Hans.md` 提交合并请求。

### 标签必须满足的条件

以下来自标准本身：

1. 只陈述对象的事实，不做「好用」「推荐」之类的评价。
2. 不设否定标签：没有「不免费」，也没有「没有 iOS 应用」。
3. 含义只解释标签本身。使用者怎么判断哪个对象该加这个标签、怎么显示，由使用者自己决定。
4. 图形要正确表达含义，一眼就能认出来，小到 14 像素也一样：圆角正方形、矢量图，不用文字字符，不用任何公司的标志。

### 怎么决定

- Nav.cc 审阅每个提议，可以采纳、改写后采纳、暂缓或不采纳。Nav.cc 有最终决定权。
- 采纳的新标签取下一个空着的 TID。编号和 TID 永不重复使用。
- 本仓库的文件由 Nav.cc 自己的标准副本生成。合并请求被采纳时，Nav.cc 在源头修改后重新生成本仓库，所以合并请求可能会被关闭而不是直接合并。带来这项修改的提交会注明作者。

### 贡献内容的许可

开 Issue 或提交合并请求，即表示你同意所贡献的内容按本仓库的许可 CC BY 4.0 授权，并同意 Nav.cc 把它作为标准的一部分发布，标准署名为 Nav.cc。
